How to play

You get a random starting word. Type a new word that begins with the last letter of the one before it, then keep going.

apple›eagle›eel

Apple ends in E, so the next word starts with E. Eagle also ends in E, so eel is allowed next.

Rules

  • It has to be a real English word. Plurals and past tenses count if the dictionary has them.
  • Letters only. No spaces, numbers or symbols.
  • You can't use the same word twice in one game.

The clock

Every word has a countdown. It starts at 15 seconds and gets a little shorter with each word you add, down to 7. If it runs out, you lose one of your three lives and the clock restarts. Lose all three and the game is over.

Score

One point per word. The word you start with doesn't count.

A small tip

Look at how your word ends before you send it. Finishing on a Q or an X makes the next turn hard.

Accounts

Without an account you can play, and only your best score is remembered. With one you also get recent games, play stats and daily streaks.
