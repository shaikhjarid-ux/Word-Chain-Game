# Word Chain 🎮

> **👉 Play the live game here: [word-chain-game.pages.dev](https://word-chain-game.pages.dev/)**[cite: 1]

A simple, fast-paced word chain game built fully with HTML. The goal is easy: type a word that starts with the last letter of the previous word before the clock runs out![cite: 1]

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. **The Chain Rule:** Start with the given word, then enter a new valid word that begins with the **last letter** of the previous one.[cite: 1]
   * *Example:* `apple` $\rightarrow$ `eagle` $\rightarrow$ `elephant`[cite: 1]
2. **No Repeating:** Words used earlier in the game cannot be repeated.[cite: 1]
3. **Beat the Clock:** You get 15 seconds per turn (and it speeds up as your chain grows!). Running out of time costs you 1 life. Lose 3 lives and it's game over.[cite: 1]
4. **Scoring:** Score points for every valid word added to the chain. Longer chains mean higher scores![cite: 1]

---

## ⚠️ Notes & Disclaimers

- **Word Validation:** Words are checked against an open-source English dictionary list[cite: 1]. Obscure words might occasionally not be recognized.
- **Browser Storage:** Your high scores and preferences are saved locally in your browser. Clearing your browser data will reset your stats.
- **Fair Play:** The game is meant for quick brain practice to play solo or with friends, don't ruin the fun by cheating![cite: 1]
